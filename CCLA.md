# Uptime Cairn Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Uptime Cairn (the "Project"),
which is owned and managed by **Webloom Labs (Pvt) Ltd** (the "Company").

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement (the "Agreement") lets an entity
(the "Corporation") Submit Contributions to the Company, authorise Contributions
Submitted by its designated employees, and grant copyright and patent licences in
them. It protects the Corporation as a contributor as well as the Company and the
Project's users. It does not transfer copyright: the Corporation keeps full
copyright in its Contributions and remains free to use them for any other
purpose.

Individuals contributing on their own behalf should sign the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) instead.

## How to sign

1. Complete the details below, sign the Agreement, and complete Schedule A.
2. Email a scanned copy to [shakil@webloomlabs.net](mailto:shakil@webloomlabs.net).
3. The Company confirms receipt and adds the GitHub usernames on Schedule A to
   the CLA bot's allowlist, so your designated employees are not asked to sign
   individually.

To add or remove designated employees later, send an updated Schedule A signed by
an authorised representative of the Corporation.
