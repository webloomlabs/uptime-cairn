# Maintainers & Committers

The authoritative roster. Roles and the process for joining or leaving are
defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Maintainers

Committers who additionally hold administrative control — repository and
organisation ownership, domains, registry namespaces, signing keys, release
infrastructure, and the security contact.
