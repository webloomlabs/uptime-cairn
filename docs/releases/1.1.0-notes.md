## Uptime Cairn 1.1.0 — reporting

Scheduled, branded uptime and SLA reports, built into the same single binary.

### Reporting

- **Five report types:** SLA/SLO (target vs. actual, error budget, burn rate,
  breach log), uptime summary, incident post-mortem (MTTD/MTTA/MTTR),
  comparative, and a custom builder.
- **PDF, HTML, CSV and JSON**, rendered in pure Go: no headless browser, no
  sidecar.
- **Scheduled delivery** by email, Slack, webhook or S3 drop, with retries and
  visible failures.
- **White-label brand profiles**, and **revocable share links** that serve the
  stored report, never a re-render.
- **Figures that show their working:** every report prints its denominator and
  maintenance policy.
- **Certificate and domain expiry calendar**, as a screen and as a report
  section.
- **History over any past range**, labelled with the resolution actually used.

### Also new

- Native **Pushover**, **Mattermost** and **Google Chat** channels.
- `cairn config validate`, `cairn version`, and `cairn import kuma -report-json`.
- gRPC v1.83.2 for CVE-2026-84445.

### Upgrading

Replace the binary or image; migrations run at start-up. **Add
`<data-dir>/reports/` to your backups** (database first, then the directory).
This release freezes the reporting endpoints under `/api/v1`: from here on
they only change in backward-compatible ways.
